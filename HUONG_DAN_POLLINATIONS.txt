HUONG DAN KET NOI NHIT AI VOI POLLINATIONS

1) Dang ky/dang nhap: https://enter.pollinations.ai/
2) Tao Secret Key (bat dau bang sk_). Khong dung pk_ lam token goi API.
3) Tao key chi cap quyen model can dung: text, image, video. Dat budget/expiry thap de thu nghiem.
4) Tren may chu, tao file .env tu .env.example va dat:
   POLLINATIONS_API_KEY=sk_xxxxxxxxx
5) Khong dua .env vao GitHub hoac static/index.html.
6) Cai dat:
   pip install -r requirements.txt
7) Chay:
   uvicorn server:app --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8000
8) Mo tren Android bang dia chi HTTPS cua may chu.
9) Bam 'Tai danh muc model' trong NHIT AI, chon dung model ID hien thi cho tung loai tac vu.
10) Chi dung model co gia 0 Pollen neu muon thu mien phi. Kiem tra gia truoc moi lan tao.

Luu y:
- API key can thiet de tao noi dung; tai khoan khong dong nghia voi su dung mien phi vo han.
- Model va gia co the thay doi.
- Video co the can cho lau; ban MVP hien tai goi video dong bo va co the timeout voi clip lau.
- SRT trong ban MVP chia thoi gian deu, chua tu dong can khop am thanh.
- Tai lieu: https://gen.pollinations.ai/docs
